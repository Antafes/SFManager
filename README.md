# Satisfactory Factory Manager

This is a tool for the game Satisfactory allowing you to organize and manage your factory.

## Features

- **Multiple factories**: create, open and delete separate factories.
- **Save import**: import a factory from a Satisfactory save file (`.sav`). Recipe machines (like constructors and 
  fabricators), miners, oil pumps, resource well pressurizers and extractors, and geothermal generators are recognized. 
  Machines that cannot be mapped are reported as skipped.
- **Productions**: manage productions per factory with building, recipe, group, position, clock speed (overclocking),
  somersloops.
- **Resource buildings**: miners, oil pumps, well extractors and geothermal generators with selectable material and
  purity. Well extractors are linked to a well pressurizer.
- **Power**: power generators including the nuclear power plant, and power consumption of all buildings.
- **Groups**: productions are grouped, the group name is prefilled and can be filtered or overwritten.
- **Bulk editing**: change several productions at once.
- **Factory overview**: per-material production, usage, unused amount and consumption with a status, plus power
  production and consumption.
- **Storage**: track materials that are stored and the productions that supply them.
- **Planning**: calculate the machines, inputs, outputs, raw materials, side products and power needed for a target
  item and rate, with alternate recipes, and create productions straight from a plan.
- **Sortable tables**: tables sort by numeric value, not by text.
- **Copy to clipboard**: copy the text of a table cell with Ctrl+C.
- **Languages**: English and German.
